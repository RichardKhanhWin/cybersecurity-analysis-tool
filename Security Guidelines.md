## Turn off RDP on your workstation when not needed.

To turn off RDP:
1. Enter 'Remote Desktop Settings' in the Windows search bar.
2. Switch 'Remote Desktop' to 'Off'.

## Don't use Telnet to connect to other computers remotely.

To turn off Telnet:
1. Enter 'Turn Windows features on or off' in the Windows search bar.
2. Uncheck Telnet Client and click 'OK'.
